## Question

Does changing only `FallMax` from `2.0` to `2.25` improve YoulossTM results in a short, paired engine match? The current candidate is the 2.25 build; the control will be compiled from the same dirty source snapshot with only that constant changed to 2.0. `HorizonOpening=32.0`, `StabilityMin=0.70`, all other source, NNUE, compiler settings, and PGO procedure are held constant.

This is a development screen, not NNUE training or tournament-control evidence. It cannot establish absolute playing strength or small Elo changes.

## Analysis and decision rule

Report wins/draws/losses, score, score by color, paired opening-cluster mean and 95% paired-*t* interval, engine-time profile, and all incidents. The 100-game screen has limited power and screens for a clear effect only.

- Advance 2.25 to a separately preregistered, disjoint-seed validation only if its paired 95% interval is wholly above 50% and the run has no clock or engine incidents.
- Prefer 2.0 if the interval is wholly below 50%.
- Otherwise call the result inconclusive and make no strength or tuning claim. Do not extend this sample after looking at results.

Any game loss or draw analysis is hypothesis generation only; it cannot change this fixed sample or its decision rule.
